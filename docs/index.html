Eliminating child care deserts in New York State

The minimum-cost plan from a mixed-integer program (920,756 variables, solved with Gurobi) that brings every ZIP code up to the state's child care coverage requirements. Click anywhere on the map to see one ZIP's numbers. Columbia IEOR E4004 course project; code and write-up on GitHub.

Part 2 ("realistic") solution: new facilities only on 215,400 candidate sites, at least 0.06 miles from any other facility in the same ZIP; expansions capped at 20% with rising per-slot costs. Requirements: slots for ½ of children aged 0–12 in high-demand ZIPs (⅓ elsewhere), and for ⅔ of children aged 0–5. Known modeling limitations are listed in the README.
